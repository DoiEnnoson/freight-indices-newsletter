WCI, SCFI & IACI: Weekly Container Freight Indices

Three indices. One weekly overview. Every Friday.

What you get

Track three major container freight indices in one place: the Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the Shanghai Containerized Freight Index (SCFI) and the Drewry Intra-Asia Container Index (IACI).

Every Friday, receive an email with the latest available figures, charts showing how the indices have developed, and a summary table comparing week-on-week and year-on-year changes.

The three indices cover different parts of the container shipping market. Bringing them together in one weekly report makes it easier to compare price movements across routes and regions.
