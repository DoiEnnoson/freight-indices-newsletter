Weekly Freight Indices Newsletter

WCI, SCFI & IACI by email every Friday

What you get

One email per week, on Fridays, with the latest values of the key container freight indices: Drewry World Container Index (WCI), Shanghai Containerized Freight Index (SCFI) and Drewry Intra-Asia Container Index (IACI). It includes charts of their development and a table with week-over-week and year-over-year changes. The newsletter is free of charge.

How to subscribe

  1. Open the form, enter your email address and confirm your consent.
  2. Within a day you will receive a confirmation email. Simply reply to it.
  3. You will receive the newsletter from the following Friday.
Open signup form

The form is provided by Google Forms. See the privacy notice for details.

Unsubscribe

Every newsletter contains an “Unsubscribe” link at the bottom. Alternatively, send an email with the subject Unsubscribe to doisnews@gmail.com.
