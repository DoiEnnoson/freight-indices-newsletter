Data processed

For the newsletter, your email address, the time of your signup, your consent and the times of confirmation and unsubscription are stored. No other data is collected.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is used solely to send you the weekly newsletter and to be able to demonstrate your consent. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Storing the times of confirmation and unsubscription serves to demonstrate consent pursuant to Art. 7(1) GDPR.

Confirmation of your signup (double opt-in)

After signing up you will receive an email, which you confirm by replying to it. Only then will the newsletter be sent to you. This ensures that nobody can sign up other people's addresses.

Retention

Your address is stored for as long as you are subscribed. Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 30 days. After you unsubscribe, nothing more will be sent to you. The entry is kept for up to three years to demonstrate consent and withdrawal and is deleted afterwards.

Unsubscribing and withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time, via the “Unsubscribe” link in every newsletter or by sending an email with the subject Unsubscribe to doisnews@gmail.com.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent you have given. An informal email to the address above is sufficient. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
